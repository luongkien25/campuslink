4. KET LUAN THEO DUNG DINH NGHIA SRS VA PHAN TICH GIOI HAN (KHONG SUY DIEN QUA MUC)
================================================================================
1. Ket luan NFR-01 (p95 redirect <= 500 ms o ~30 RPS qua Internet): KHONG DAT.
   - Theo dung dinh nghia SRS (do phia may phat qua Internet tu Viet Nam toi us-east-1, moi request tao ket noi HTTPS moi), p95 redirect dat 1.080,7 ms (16/11), ~1.200 ms (17/11) va 2.674 ms (18/11), deu vuot nguong 500 ms.
   - So do phia server tren Host A (log Nginx $request_time, chi la so do chan doan, khong thay the dinh nghia NFR-01) dat 238 ms (16/11) va 256 ms (17/11) o muc dat 30 RPS, 74-117 ms o 10 RPS (18/11), nhung len toi 596 ms o luot doi chung 18/11 (W6). Vi vay khong the ket luan chung la "p95 phia server luon <= 500 ms".

2. Ket luan NFR-02 (ty le 5xx/timeout < 1% trong luot khong gay loi): DAT.
   - Tat ca cac luot khong gay loi deu co 0 HTTP 5xx va ty le client timeout 599 chi tu 0,00% den 0,154% (thap hon nhieu so voi nguong 1%). Ngay ca trong luot failover tat app VM A (18/11), ty le timeout trong 301,4 s A tat cung chi la 2/3.015 = 0,066% (0 HTTP 5xx).

3. Ket luan NFR-03 (Workload 80/15/5 o 10/30/60 RPS): DAT MOT PHAN (thieu muc 60 RPS on dinh).
   - Da chay day du profile 80/15/5 o muc 10 RPS (18/11) va 30 RPS (16/11, 17/11, 18/11).
   - Ly do thieu muc 60 RPS: Hai lan thu dat 60 RPS ngay 17/11 (B1 32 luong, B2 160 luong) chi dat thong luong thuc te ~32-33 RPS va phai dung som. Phep do ramp chi-redirect bang ApacheBench keep-alive truc tiep tu Host A qua VIP noi bo ngay 20/11 (PR #88) xac nhan thong luong toi da cua cum dung o ~32,1-32,8 RPS tu muc concurrency 8 den 64.

4. Quan sat tuong quan & ung vien nguyen nhan (chua chung minh nhan qua):
   - Trong log Nginx tren Host A, upstream_response_time (urt) xap xi request_time (rt) (lech toi da ~1 ms), cho thay do tre phia server nam o doan sau Nginx (VIP -> app VM -> DB VM).
   - So lieu Gnocchi cpu rate:mean (60 s) cho thay khi p95 phia server tang len 596 ms (18/11 W6) va trong suot 5 muc ramp 20/11 (~32,5 RPS), CPU cua DB VM campus-db (1 vCPU) o muc 92-98%, trong khi CPU moi app VM chi o muc 27-51,5%.
   - Kiem chi doc tren campus-db ngay 20/11 (#76, PR #84) loai tru backup va autovacuum dai trung 14:32Z ngay 18/11, dong thoi ghi nhan Shlink mo ket noi PostgreSQL TCP/TLSv1.3 + SCRAM moi cho tung request (~50 phien moi/giay o ~25 visit/giay). Day la quan sat tuong quan va ung vien nguyen nhan cho viec CPU DB VM 1 vCPU cham tran o ~32,5 RPS; chua thuc hien thi nghiem thay doi cau hinh (nhu connection pooling / pgbouncer) trong giai doan freeze nen khong khang dinh nhan qua.
